A little ready goes a long way

Feel ready for whatever comes.

Disasters can feel overwhelming. Getting prepared doesn’t have to. This guide helps households in Philippine communities take a few simple steps to feel more ready.

A plan that sticks

Make a plan your people can remember.

A quick conversation now can make it easier to find each other and get help later.

01

Choose two meeting spots

Pick one close to home and another outside your neighborhood in case you can’t get back.

02

Pick an out-of-area contact

Choose someone everyone can check in with if local phone lines are busy or you get separated.

03

Know your local alerts and routes

Find trusted local updates, evacuation routes, and the safest place to go for the hazards in your area.

04

Share it, then practice

Save the plan somewhere everyone can find it. Talk through what you’d do, including how to help anyone who needs extra support.

If you need to evacuate

Go early, follow local instructions.

For typhoons, floods, earthquakes, volcanic activity, or other emergencies, evacuation advice depends on your location and the hazard.

In an emergency: follow announcements from your barangay and local government. Call the Philippines emergency hotline at 911 if you need urgent assistance.

Stay informed, stay flexible.

Learn which local authorities provide emergency alerts in your area. During an emergency, follow their instructions—conditions and safe routes can change quickly.
